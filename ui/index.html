      Let I be the candidate ZIP areas, and let xᵢ be dollars allocated to area i. The assumed benefit rᵢ is projected charging sessions per year per dollar, estimated from your demand proxy. The cap Uᵢ is the maximum spend you assume each area can absorb.

      maximize     ∑ᵢ∈I rᵢ xᵢ
  subject to   ∑ᵢ∈I xᵢ ≤ 1,000,000
               0 ≤ xᵢ ≤ Uᵢ     for every i ∈ I
               xᵢ ∈ ℝ         for every i ∈ I

      This LP allocates dollars. An installation plan can use integer port counts and binary site-opening variables. A stochastic model can choose the same build across all demand scenarios and score its expected benefit.
