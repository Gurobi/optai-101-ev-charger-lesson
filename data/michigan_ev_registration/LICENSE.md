## AFDC state registration totals

Covers `michigan_ev_registration_state_totals_afdc.csv`.

- **Source:** U.S. Department of Energy, Alternative Fuels Data Center,
  [2025 Light-Duty Vehicle Registration Counts by State and Fuel Type](https://afdc.energy.gov/vehicle-registration).
  The National Laboratory of the Rockies derived the rounded counts from
  Experian Information Solutions data. All 12 Michigan values were checked
  against the published table on 2026-11-20.
- **Redistribution basis:** This CSV transcribes the year, standard fuel
  category names, and 12 published Michigan counts using the lesson's own
  schema. U.S. copyright does not protect individual facts. This basis
  covers the factual extract and relies on neither AFDC's download terms
  nor a license to the underlying Experian dataset.
  [U.S. Copyright Office, Circular 33](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ33.pdf).
